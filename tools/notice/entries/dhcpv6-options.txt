Packs dhcpv6-options, edns0-options; modules dhcpv6, edns0
  IETF RFC 9915 (DHCPv6, section 21) and RFC 6891 (EDNS(0), section 6.1.2),
  https://www.rfc-editor.org/ (IETF Trust, BCP 78). Option formats and sizes
  restated. Option codes and names from the IANA registries DHCPv6 Option
  Codes and DNS EDNS0 Option Codes (OPT), CC0 1.0 per the IANA and IETF joint
  statement of 10 November 2021.
