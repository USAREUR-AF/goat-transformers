# GMTI packet layout (AEDP-4607 Ed A V1), stated in this repository's own words and names.
# One row per field, in wire order. Byte sizes; every field is byte aligned, most significant bit first.
# kind: header (common to every packet) or a segment kind of tools/gmti/kinds.tsv.
# bit: the field's position in the segment's existence mask, counted from 1 in reading order; empty when the segment has no mask.
# form: A text | I unsigned | S two's complement | E enumeration | FL flags | SA signed binary angle (180/2^n deg)
#       | BA binary angle (360/2^n deg) | BP sign-magnitude fixed point of a non-negative quantity (B16: 7 fraction bits, B32: 23)
#       | SM sign-magnitude fixed point of a signed quantity (B16: 7 fraction bits, B32: 16), read with `smscale` | skip (mask bit named, nothing read) | rest (the remainder of the segment, not read)
# bind: a shared key and its transform, written in place of a raw key.  also: further keys on the same bits (`key via table`, `;` between).
