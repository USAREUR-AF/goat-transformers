## What is not carried

A collection description of Common Part 2. No shared key is bound (metadata, not a reported
entity). Extents (`bbox`, `storageCrsBbox`, `interval`) are arrays of arrays; a `crs`, a
`storageCrs` or a `trs` may be a string or an object; grid resolutions and coordinates may be
a number or a string: none of these is mapped.
