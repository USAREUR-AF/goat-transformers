## Evidence for the shared keys

- `time_ms` (`issuanceTime`): the time the office issued the product, a date-time; `iso8601_ms` reads it. As for the `sent` time of an alert (`nws-alert`), the issuance time is the time the text holds from.

## Not bound

The product text (`productText`) is one string, a raw key; the product is identified by its code and issuing office, not by an entity identifier.
