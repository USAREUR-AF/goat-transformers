## What the message is

The pack's message is one Foxglove schema message of the type named above, in its protobuf
encoding: the bytes of one message on a channel of an MCAP file, or of one message sent over the
Foxglove WebSocket protocol, where the channel names the schema. Two other packs read the same
schemas: `foxglove-location-fix` and `foxglove-location-fixes` (protobuf, generated by
`tools/gen/protobuf-2-run.sh`, with the position of a fix bound to shared keys), and
`ros2-foxglove-msgs` (the ROS 2 form of the schemas).

The schema files were read for names, field numbers and types only (`--no-comments`); the notes
here are this repository's own words.

## Everything raw

These schemas describe robotics visualization data: poses, transforms and primitives in a named
coordinate frame (metres and quaternions relative to that frame), images, point clouds, audio,
log lines and annotations. None of them gives a latitude and longitude, names a thing that
is being tracked, or gives a speed over the ground, so no field is bound to a shared key and the
packs convert to no other format. Every field the protobuf wire can state is a raw key of the
pack's own module.

- Times are `google.protobuf.Timestamp` (and durations `google.protobuf.Duration`): two raw
  integer keys each, seconds and nanoseconds. No transform joins them into `time_ms`.
- `bytes` fields (image, audio, video and point-cloud data, model files) and `fixed32` fields
  (image, grid and voxel dimensions, strides and offsets, the vertex indices of line and triangle
  primitives, audio sample rates and channel counts, a log line number) have no
  transform on this wire and are not carried.
- The `GeoJSON` schema holds a GeoJSON document as one string; the pack carries the string and
  does not parse it.
- Messages that nest others carry them as `group` blocks; a repeated number is written packed.
