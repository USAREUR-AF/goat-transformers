LicenseRef-APRS-NC: the permission statement of the APRS Protocol Reference

The packs under this label restate the APRS Protocol Reference, Protocol
Version 1.0, document version 1.0.1 (APRS101.PDF, 29 August 2000), by the APRS
Working Group. Its permission statement reads:

  This document may be copied for non-commercial purposes only, and must
  include the above copyright statement and trademark statements in full.

The copyright and trademark statements it refers to are quoted in the
PROVENANCE.md of the pack.
