LicenseRef-MS-OpenSpec

The source from which this pack's layout was taken is Microsoft Open Specifications documentation. Its
"Intellectual Property Rights Notice for Open Specifications Documentation" lets anyone make copies of
the documentation to develop implementations of the technologies it describes, and distribute portions
of it in those implementations or in their documentation. It states that it grants no licence under
Microsoft's patents; a document may separately be covered by the Microsoft Open Specifications Promise or
Community Promise, and other patent licences are available from Microsoft. All other rights are reserved.
The notice is printed at the front of each document and on its page under
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/.

This repository restates the layout in its own words (field names, sizes, codes, notes and vectors); it
copies no text, table or figure of the documentation. That restatement is offered under the repository's
licence. Whether a use of the protocol needs a patent licence from Microsoft is not settled by the notice;
each pack's PROVENANCE.md says whether its document is on the Open Specifications Promise's list. Treat
commercial use of a pack with this label as unknown until that is decided.
