## What this repository takes

The OBIS reduced codes of the P1 objects, their value formats and units (sections 6.4 and 6.12),
and the shapes of the header and end lines. The example telegram of section 6.13 is not copied;
the vectors use lines written for them (`tools/gen/dsmr-vectors.py`).
