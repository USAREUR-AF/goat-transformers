## Licence

The Video Services Forum states that its Technical Recommendations are freely available; the document says:

> © 2024 Video Services Forum
>
> This work is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International License. To
> view a copy of this license, visit https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/4.0/

Under rule 17 of this project a CC BY-ND document that is implemented, not adapted, gives an Apache-2.0 pack
with the ND notice quoted: the document is not modified or redistributed, and the pack restates its packet
layouts in this project's words. The pack keeps the default Apache-2.0 label.

The document draws attention to a patent claim concerning its sections 5 (except 5.6.4), 6 and 7, whose holder
has assured the Forum it will license it to members and non-members implementing the Recommendation;
information from VIDEO-FLOW LTD, as the document states.

This pack restates the layouts (field order, sizes, codes) in its own words; no text, figure, table or
code of the sources is copied. The vectors are packets built by `tools/gen/media-vectors.py` from values
chosen there; their expected bytes are derived there, without an engine.
