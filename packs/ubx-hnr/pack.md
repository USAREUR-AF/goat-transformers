## The message

`wire bits`, `bit_order lsb`, `word_bytes 1`; hex text. One UBX frame without
its checksum; numbers are little-endian:

| Bytes | Content | In the pack |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | sync characters 0xB5 0x62 | common `when` |
| 2 | class, 0x28 | common `when` |
| 3 | id | per-kind `when` |
| 4-5 | payload length in bytes | per-kind `when` = the definition's fixed length |
| 6 … | payload | the kind's `field` lines, at their offsets |

The message is the frame without its checksum, in both directions: decode takes
the sync characters, header and payload, and encode writes them. A framer adds
the two checksum bytes before sending and checks them on receipt: an 8-bit
Fletcher checksum over class, id, length and payload (a running sum A of the
bytes and a running sum B of A, each modulo 256), written after the payload.
That is the item "Layered wires and framing" of `docs/roadmap.md`. A message
whose identity includes the first payload byte (the MGA messages) has a per-kind
`when` on that byte. Where the source chooses among definitions of one identity
by the value of one payload byte (`ubxvariants.py`: a version or type byte), the
kind it chooses that way has a `when` on that byte; the other definitions of the
identity are told apart by their length.

### Core keys

pyubx2 states a scale for a field but rarely its unit or meaning. A field binds
a core or track key only where a public source shows both:

- ublox_msgs (`https://github.com/KumarRobotics/ublox`, package `ublox_msgs`, commit `3a3e1c253722`, BSD;
  the commit the ros2-ublox-msgs pack uses): the .msg file of the message states
  the unit of the field in its comment, and says what the field is (the north,
  east or down velocity, the 2-D ground speed, the 2-D heading of motion, the
  latitude, longitude or height above the ellipsoid). The generator reads the
  file and binds a field only when the comment says so and states the unit the
  table below converts: mm/s or cm/s to m/s, 1e-5 degrees to degrees, 1e-7
  degrees to degrees, mm to m. Up is the negative of down. A heading of motion
  binds `course`, read as degrees from true north, as for a GNSS solution in a
  north-east-down frame.

| Kind | Field | Key | Transform | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hnr_pvt` | `lon` | `lon` | `sscale:1/10000000` | HnrPVT.msg `lon` [deg / 1e-7] |
| `hnr_pvt` | `lat` | `lat` | `sscale:1/10000000` | HnrPVT.msg `lat` [deg / 1e-7] |
| `hnr_pvt` | `height` | `hae` | `sscale:1/1000` | HnrPVT.msg `height` [mm] |
| `hnr_pvt` | `gSpeed` | `speed_ms` | `sscale:1/1000` | HnrPVT.msg `g_speed` [mm/s] |
| `hnr_pvt` | `headMot` | `course` | `sscale:1/100000` | HnrPVT.msg `head_mot` [deg / 1e-5] |

Every other field stays a raw key, accuracies and times among them: the
sources above state no unit for it, or state one this pack does not convert.

### `requires`

Each kind requires up to 3 of its own keys, so an entity from another format
selects a kind only when it holds them.

## What is not mapped

- The checksum (see The message), and frames whose payload length is not the
  definition's fixed length: such a frame selects no kind (`no-kind`).
- Enumerations are carried as integers; the bits of a bit field that the
  definition does not name are not mapped.

## Vectors

`vectors/rt-<class><id>-<kind>.json`: one same-format round trip per kind, the
frame written by a UBX serializer in the generator from sample values; a kind
with a counted group has one with two items and one with none (`-no-items`).

3 vectors.
