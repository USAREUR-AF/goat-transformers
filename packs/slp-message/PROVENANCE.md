## Licence

RFC 2608 states "Copyright (C) The Internet Society (1999). All Rights Reserved." and its distribution is
unlimited; it is subject to BCP 78 and the IETF Trust's Legal Provisions Relating to IETF Documents
(https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info). The pack keeps the default Apache-2.0 label.

This pack restates the layouts (field order, sizes, codes) in its own words; no text, figure or table of
the sources is copied. The vectors are packets built by `tools/gen/dirnaming-vectors.py` from values chosen
there; their expected bytes are derived there, without an engine.
