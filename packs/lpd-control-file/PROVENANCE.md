## Licence

RFC 1179 is an informational RFC from before the IETF Trust; it carries no copyright line, and its status
section states that its distribution is unlimited. RFCs are covered by BCP 78 and the IETF Trust's Legal
Provisions Relating to IETF Documents (https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info). The pack keeps the default
Apache-2.0 label.

This pack restates the layouts (codes, operands, order) in its own words; no text, figure or table of the
source is copied. The vectors are built by `tools/gen/printing-vectors.py` from values chosen there; their
expected bytes are derived there, without an engine.
