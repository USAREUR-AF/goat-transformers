## Licence

The RFCs are IETF documents. Each carries the IETF Trust copyright and refers to BCP 78 and the
IETF Trust's Legal Provisions Relating to IETF Documents (https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info).
This pack restates the layouts (field order, sizes, values) in its own words; no text, figure or
example message of the RFCs is copied. The vectors are messages built by
`tools/gen/routing-vectors.py` from values chosen there; their expected bytes are derived there,
without an engine.
