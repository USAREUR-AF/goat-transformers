Neither edition states a licence. This project's standing decision is that a standard whose text
reserves all rights, without limiting the purpose of use, is restated from it; the layouts (field
order, sizes, codes) are restated here in this project's own words, and no text, figure or table of
the specification is copied. Because no licence is stated the pack is labelled `LicenseRef-unstated`
(REUSE.toml in this directory; model/gtpu.def.license for the module), which keeps it out of the
`open` profile; the choice is recorded for the operator, who can overturn it.

bytes are derived there, without an engine.
