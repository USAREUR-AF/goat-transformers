## What this repository takes

The SML_Message structure, the messageBody tags, the structures of SML_PublicOpen.Req and .Res,
SML_PublicClose.Req and .Res, SML_GetList.Res, SML_ListEntry, SML_Value and SML_Time, and the
TL-field coding (sections 5 and 6 of the document). The vectors are built by
`tools/gen/sml-vectors.py`.
