## Licence

The RFC is an IETF document under the IETF Trust copyright, BCP 78 and the IETF Trust's Legal
Provisions Relating to IETF Documents (https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info). This pack restates the
layouts (field order, sizes, codes) in its own words; no text, figure or example of the RFC is
copied. The pack keeps the default Apache-2.0 label. The vectors are messages built by
`tools/gen/telecom-vectors.py` from values chosen there; their expected bytes are derived there,
without an engine.
