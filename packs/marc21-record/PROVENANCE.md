## Licence

The pages are published by the Network Development and MARC Standards Office of the Library of Congress,
which maintains the MARC 21 formats together with Library and Archives Canada. The pages read carry no
copyright notice and no licence. The office's questions page (https://www.loc.gov/marc/faq.html, read
2026-11-24) says excerpts may be used with credit; the credit is given below. Because the formats are
maintained with a second country's library, this project does not rely on their being a work of the United
States Government alone. Only facts of the layout are taken. The pack keeps the default Apache-2.0 label.

This pack restates the character positions in its own words; no text or table of the pages is copied. The
vectors are built by `tools/gen/biblio-vectors.py` from values chosen there (invented records, with the
record length, base address, field lengths and starting positions computed there); their expected bytes
are derived there, without an engine.
