# Provenance of the cef pack

The pack and the module `cef` are written by hand from the section "CEF Implementation" of the
format guide; the vectors by `tools/gen/logs-vectors.py` from records written for them. No part
of the guide is kept in this repository. Read on 2026-11-20.
